What we do

DATA

Sourcing and integrity

Market data arrives messy: gaps in the calendar, duplicated ticks, prices that contradict themselves. We build the ingestion and audit layer that catches those faults before anyone draws a conclusion from them.

RESEARCH

Risk measurement

Volatility, drawdown, return distribution, tail behaviour. We compute the figures that describe what an asset actually did, with the assumptions behind each one written down rather than buried.

TOOLING

Research applications

Dashboards and internal tools that put the analysis in front of the people making the decision — interactive, reproducible, and documented well enough that someone else can check the work.

02

Our work

Quartermaster

A quantitative research dashboard for crypto assets. It sources historical market data, audits it for integrity faults, and halts rather than charting anything that fails inspection. What survives is rendered as risk and return: volatility, drawdown, and the fat-tailed return distributions that summary statistics tend to hide.

The full methodology is published — every formula, every limitation, every assumption — so the output can be checked rather than trusted.
