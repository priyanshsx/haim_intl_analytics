How the firm works

Haim International is deliberately small. Work is done directly by the person who signs it, which means the methodology can always be explained by whoever produced it.

The operating principle is that data integrity comes before analysis. Market data arrives with gaps, duplicates, and contradictory ticks. Our tools audit before they compute, and they stop rather than charting something that failed inspection. A clean-looking chart built on corrupted data is worse than no chart at all, because it looks trustworthy.

What we publish

Every figure we put in front of someone comes with its method: the formula, the data source, the assumptions, and the limitations. Where a calculation is an approximation, we say so. Where a data source is imperfect, we name the imperfection.

The Quartermaster methodology is the clearest example — every calculation written out in full, with a limitations section near the top rather than buried at the bottom, and a changelog recording corrections rather than quietly amending them.
